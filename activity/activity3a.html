Introduction of Paragraph Tag

In HTML, paragraphs are defined with the p tag. A paragraph element in an HTML documents is made up of content such as text, images, or other content which appears between opening p tag and closing p tag.

p tag is the HTML tag for a paragraph. Additing the p tag to an HTML doc, followed by text, will create a paragraph on your page. Additing the closing p tag will end the paragraph.
